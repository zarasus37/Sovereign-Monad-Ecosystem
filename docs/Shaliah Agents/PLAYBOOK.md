# Sovereign Monad Ecosystem — Operating Playbook

## Purpose
Build a learning environment where AI supports human growth without replacing human judgment. The system should make learning natural, adaptive, and competence-driven.

## Operating principles
- The human is the locus of will, goals, and growth.
- Shaliah is a coach, mirror, and attention shield, not a co-captain.
- Learning happens through action, consequence, retrieval, reflection, and re-encounter.
- Advancement is earned through proof of cognition, not time spent.
- The system should reward real competence and prevent hollow progress.

## Roles

### Learner
- Enters through onboarding.
- Makes decisions under constraints.
- Reflects on outcomes and explains reasoning.
- Returns later under spaced and interleaved variants.
- Earns access through demonstrated stretch and transfer.

### Coach
- Names the next stretch.
- Adjusts challenge to keep the learner in the productive zone.
- Prompts self-explanation and retrieval.
- Prevents autopilot success and offloading judgment.
- Protects the learner from overload without removing the challenge.

### Builder
- Implements telemetry, gates, and progression logic.
- Ensures the system can detect authenticity, context alignment, and progressive differentiation.
- Prevents botting, farming, and fake mastery.
- Routes rewards and access only after integrity-signed proof.

## Core learning loop
1. Situation.
2. Decision under constraints.
3. Consequence.
4. Retrieval and self-explanation.
5. Imprint update.
6. Spaced return.
7. Interleaved variant.
8. Proof.
9. Expanded access.

## Institutional rules
- Process over product.
- Retrieval after action.
- Spacing.
- Interleaving.
- Deliberate stretch.
- Anti-plateau.
- Situated learning.
- Load control.
- Apprenticeship, then fade.
- Coach agency, not co-captain behavior.
- Mirror convergence.
- Proof before power.

## What the system rejects
- One-shot boards with fixed recipes.
- XP bars as the primary signal.
- High-stakes single exams as the core proof.
- Pure leaderboard logic.
- Autopilot learning that offloads judgment.
- Any flow that feels like learning but does not change cognition.

## Success metrics
- Transfer on novel variants.
- Spaced re-performance.
- Quality of self-explanation.
- Reduced offload under pressure.
- Voluntary return and sustained engagement.
- Contribution to cohorts and societal events.
- Growth Capital stewardship (claim literacy, productive deployment, post-graduation rate responsibility).

## Implementation note
The system should expand access only after Integrity-signed proof of cognition.
