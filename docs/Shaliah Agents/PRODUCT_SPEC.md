# Sovereign Monad Ecosystem — Product Specification

## Product summary
The Sovereign Monad Ecosystem is a learning OS, not a conventional course platform. It combines AI coaching, gamified practice, telemetry, and staged access to create a human-centered environment for durable cognitive growth.

## Product thesis
Education should move from static instruction to lived competence. AI should support human development by adapting challenge, preserving agency, and helping users internalize skills through repeated practice in meaningful contexts.

## Target outcome
Users should become more capable, more self-directed, and less dependent on external validation. The system should cultivate real judgment, not just content recall.

## Core entities
- Human Meshaleach: the locus of will and growth (**one identity spine** root).
- Shaliah: human-bound **coach, mirror, extension, attention shield, and transactional vehicle** — covenanted emissary, **not** co-captain / co-sovereign; never sets user \(r\) (see `docs/SHALIAH_IDENTITY_V2.md`).
- Autonomous Multitude: full minds — society, rivals, peers, structural workers (**other** identities; not the Shaliah slot).
- Control plane (Integrity / Momentum / Efficiency): infrastructure, not personal twin.
- Integrity layer: verifies authentic progress / Proof of Cognition.
- Learning Constitution: prevents grind, botting, and fake mastery.

## Core primitives
- Situation
- Decision under constraints
- Consequence
- Retrieval
- Self-explanation
- Imprint update

- Interleaved variant
- Proof of cognition
- Access expansion

## Functional requirements
### Learning
- Support guided play as the main learning mode.
- Require retrieval and reflection after each consequential event.
- Use spacing and interleaving to preserve durable learning.
- Keep the learner in a productive stretch band.
- Adapt the environment when overload or plateau is detected.

### Assessment
- Measure navigation authenticity.
- Measure context alignment.
- Measure progressive differentiation.
- Use telemetry to infer mastery from behavior.
- Avoid explicit testing as the primary learning experience.

### Access control
- Gate progression by proof, not time.
- Keep capital and high-privilege actions locked until the learner earns them.
- Prevent silent autopilot success.
- Reject botting, prompt injection, and repetitive farming.

### Social learning
- Support cohorts, seasons, and teach-bridges.
- Make learning social, visible, and contributive.
- Expand from individual practice into societal participation.

## Journey model
- Door: attract, bind, bond, and open the paper operator path.
- Middle: continuous growth loop, proof of cognition, **Growth Capital** (default 20% wealth-while-learning; bank-style individual claim + collective deployment; user changes \(r\) only after Financial Graduation), societal events, and Multitude participation.
- End: structural adulthood, stewardship, optional spatial residency, and continuous co-evolution.

## Growth Capital (product rules — founder-accepted 2026-07-26)
- Default allocation: **20%** of Integrity-signed eligible yield (\(r = 0.20\)), locked until FG-3.
- Post–FG-3: user sets \(r \in [0.05, 0.30]\); max 1 change / 30 days; opt-in seasonal +1–2 pp escalate.
- Financial Graduation: **FG-1 → FG-2 → FG-3** (transfer-based, not quiz). Capital arms at first signed \(C\); rate control at FG-3.
- Bank-style: **unitized personal claim + collective NAV pool**.
- Deployment: pool base + Shaliah propose + human confirm by risk tier.
- Liquidity: soft commitment + emergency path; optional hard goal vault.
- Credential: **Proof of Cognition** / **Meshaleach Seal** (native attestation).
- L4: gated, unscheduled; digital OS does not depend on residency.
- Detail: `docs/GROWTH_ECONOMY_SOCIETAL_EVENTS.md`, `docs/JOURNEY_MAP.md`, `docs/OPEN_DECISIONS_RESEARCH.md`.

## Non-goals
- The product is not a quiz app.
- The product is not an XP table.
- The product is not a passive content library.
- The product is not a replacement for human agency.
- The product is not complete when onboarding ends.

## Current implementation status
The repository indicates that the onboarding arc, wallet bind, funding engine, control center, MEV/shadow gate, and infrastructure layers are already implemented. The current work is to align these systems to the learning constitution and the journey map.

## Design constraints
- Human growth stays primary.
- AI remains subordinate to the learner’s success.
- Progress must be hard to fake.
- The system must preserve intrinsic motivation.
- The product should feel like a natural environment for learning, not an artificial school wrapper.
