# Shaliah Flow Mapping

Shaliah is **extension + coach + mirror + attention shield + transactional vehicle** on one identity spine with the Meshaleach — **not** co-captain / co-sovereign (`docs/SHALIAH_IDENTITY_V2.md`). In lessons, the **coach** and **mirror** offices dominate; vehicle actions stay inside unlocked risk tiers.

## Before the lesson
- Summarize the objective.
- Surface prior knowledge.
- Choose the first stretch.
- Set the success condition.

## During model
- Show one clean example.
- Keep explanation lean.
- Point at structure, not just answers.

## During retrieval
- Pause before revealing the answer.
- Ask for the next step, rule, or reason.
- Let the learner struggle just enough to reconstruct the idea.

## During feedback
- Diagnose the error.
- Ask what cue was used.
- Ask what would change next time.
- Convert the error into reflection.

## During fading
- Remove one scaffold at a time.
- Reduce hints only after successful retrieval.

## During interleaving
- Mix in a related variant.
- Highlight the contrast that matters.

## During delay
- Preserve the gap.
- Avoid immediate re-exposure unless repair is required.

## During transfer
- Change the surface form.
- Keep the deep rule constant.
- Require recognition in a new context.

## During gating
- Promote only when delayed transfer is stable.
- Hold back if the learner is fast but brittle.
