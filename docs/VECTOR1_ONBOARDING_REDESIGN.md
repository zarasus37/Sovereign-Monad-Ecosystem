## 0. Why a Shaliah exists (load-bearing)

**Purpose:** You and the agent **grow with each other** — mutual growth, not a disposable assistant and not a co-captain rival will (`SHALIAH_IDENTITY_V2`).

AI ability to learn and personalize is **bounded by the data and experience it is allowed to form** about *this* human. If the beginning is ornamental (cosmetic puzzles, hollow XP), the partnership is fake: the agent never truly knows who it is extending, and the human never learns how to grow *with* a mind that knows them.

**Meaningful partnership requires a non-ornamental beginning:**

| Side | What they gain from Vector 1 |
|------|------------------------------|
| **Human** | Sees themselves more clearly; learns how they solve, feel under load, and communicate intent; learns how agents process and act |
| **Shaliah** | Builds a deep, load-bearing model of *this* person — who they are embodying, how they learn, what they need next, when to Stretch or scaffold |
| **Together** | A covenant where growth is mutual: PL caps ACL; coach adjusts because it *knows*; human trusts because the bond was earned through real knowing |

The beginning **serves real purpose**: foundation for everything later (command fluency, FG, seasons, vehicle power).

## 1. Foundation layer — who is this mind?

Before (and feeding) interactive Vector 1 observation, the human **imparts** structured self-knowledge. This is not a quiz for grades — it is **substrate for the Cognitive Twin** and for the agent’s lifelong coaching model.

### 1.1 Required psychometric foundation

| Instrument | Role |
|------------|------|
| **NEO-PI-3 / NEO-300-class Big Five** | Broad personality dimensions (OCEAN) — baseline temperament, style, openness to Stretch, conscientiousness under task, etc. |
| **Short Dark Triad (SD3)** | Deeper modifiers (Machiavellianism, narcissism, psychopathy traits as *profile modifiers*, never a standalone moral brand) — how pressure, status, and strategy color behavior |

Aligns with existing impartation doctrine (`SHALIAH_AGENTS.md` §4.1, §5): industrial-grade psychometrics **auto-construct** a starting build; **DSD/SD3 modifies Big Five**, never floats alone.

**Anti-gamification:** Once embedded, the twin does **not** rewrite from chat flattery. It **updates from lived interaction** (Vector 1 observation + lifelong HCD).

### 1.2 Optional depth layer — natal chart analysis

If the user opts in, a **deep analytical breakdown of their natal birth chart** may be imparted as additional symbolic-psychological structure: timing, elemental balance, aspect tensions, vocation/relationship patterns as *interpretive lenses* — not fatalism, not horoscope spam.

| Property | Rule |
|----------|------|
| **Opt-in** | Never required for door completion |
| **Depth** | Analytical breakdown worthy of the ecosystem’s bar — not a one-line sun-sign label |
| **Use** | Feeds Shaliah’s model of *who they are embodying* and how they may meet challenge; coaches Stretch with richer priors |
| **Consent** | Consent-graded data (intimacy tier); user can revoke future use per policy |
| **Not** | Replacement for behavior under task; chart is prior, Vector 1 **observation still rules** when they conflict |

This offers a level of articulated self-understanding most people never receive — and it can **all go into the agent** as part of mutual knowing.

### 1.3 What foundation is for

```text
Psychometrics (+ optional natal analysis)
        │
        ▼
  Cognitive Twin seed / impartation hash
        │
        ▼
  Vector 1 live observation (behavior under challenge)
        │
        ▼
  Twin grounded + Shaliah “knows” how to coach THIS human
        │
        ▼
  Lifelong mutual growth (difficulty, content, tone, timing)
```

## 2. Vector 1 — observe who they are under load

Vector 1 is **not** a game trophy path. It is a structured **opportunity to learn and observe** the user’s **current**:

| Observed dimension | Examples of signal |
|--------------------|--------------------|
| **Cognitive ability** | Complexity they can hold; transfer under novelty |
| **Problem-solving method** | Plan-first vs thrash; tool use; recovery after error |
| **Thought processing** | Sequence, abstraction, self-explanation quality |
| **Emotional tendencies under challenge** | Freeze, rage-quit, curiosity, shame, grit, help-seeking |
| **Communication with a processing mind** | How they form commands; how they read failure |
| **Reasoning literacy** | How they read *why* the agent acted; depth of critique vs rubber-stamp |

**Dual observers (non-negotiable):**

| Observer | Role |
|----------|------|
| **System / Integrity path** | HCD, telemetry, anti-bot, Proof of Cognition honesty |
| **Shaliah itself** | Mirror + coach model — develops a **deep understanding of who that user is, who they are embodying** |

The agent is not a passive log. It is **learning the person** so it can:

- Know **how they learn**  
- Know **what they need to learn next**  
- Know **when to adjust difficulty** (Stretch band / ZPD)  
- Know **how to speak to them** without becoming co-captain  
- Stay coupled as they change (Safety Throttle / lifelong monitoring)

**Mutual growth with someone who truly knows you — including helping you know yourself.**

|---------|---------|

| One-sided | Does not give Shaliah a deep enough model of the person |

**New bar for door exit:**  
(1) Foundation imparted (NEO + SD3; natal optional)  
(2) Shaliah has **observed** method under challenge (dual observation)  
(3) Human can **communicate** with the agent (command + shared-loop literacy)  
(4) Human can **read the agent’s thought process** after actions — reconstruct *why*, not only approve/stop  
(5) Twin is grounded in **behavior**, not only self-report

## 4. What Broken Genesis is actually for

**Not:** “Fix a broken board so the twin wakes” as a self-contained minigame win.

**Is:** Genesis is incomplete until:

1. The human has **imparted** who they are (foundation instruments + optional chart)  
2. The human and agent have **practiced the channel** — speak so a processor can act; see shared process loops  
3. The agent has **watched** the human under difficulty enough to coach as someone who knows them  

The “repair” is **mutual knowing + communicative interface** between Meshaleach and Shaliah.

### Outcomes (must be true)

1. **Mental model of agent processing**  
   User can explain (in their own words, under pressure): input → representation → constraint/check → action → observation. Not a textbook dump — demonstrated by issuing commands and reading traces.

2. **Command literacy**  
   User can write/select **clear intents** that become agent actions (with policy bounds). Ambiguous mush fails productively; structured intent succeeds.

3. **Shared-process visualization**  
   UI shows **side-by-side** (or overlay): human reasoning steps and agent pipeline steps as **functionally isomorphic** loops — so the bond is “we process,” not “I own a chatbot.”

4. **Bond without co-captain**  
   Shaliah remains extension/coach/mirror/vehicle (Identity v2). The human owns will; the agent owns execution within mandate. Communication skill is the first Stretch.

5. **Un-fakeable method capture**  
   Still capture *how* they communicate (verbose vs sparse, trial-error vs plan-first, recovery from failed parse) — that seeds the Cognitive Twin from **real dialogue/command practice**, not only circuit clicks.

## 4.1 Shared processing model (what the visual teaches)

Both human and agent run a loop. The surface differs; the **function** rhymes.

```text
HUMAN                              AGENT / MODEL
──────                             ─────────────
Sense / attend                     Receive input (prompt, tools, state)
Select relevant                    Tokenize / retrieve context
Organize into model                Build latent plan / tool args
Integrate with goals               Apply constraints / policy-as-code
Act / speak                        Call tools / emit action
Observe consequence                Observe result / errors
Update priors                      Update context / memory stream
```

**Product rule:** Every successful door exercise makes at least one of these stages **visible** on both sides. No pure black-box “it just answered.”

Stealth remains: user came to bond with an agent; they leave fluent in **how minds-in-loops work** — including their own.

## 5. Redesigned door spine (foundation + observation + fluency)

Legacy names may be retired in UI. Order is intentional.

### Phase 0 — **Impartation foundation** (before or as door opens)

| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| **NEO-PI-3 / NEO-300-class** | Big Five dimensions → Twin seed |
| **SD3** | Dark Triad short form → modifiers on Big Five |
| **Optional natal analysis** | Deep chart breakdown → symbolic-psychological priors (opt-in) |
| **Consent + hash** | Impartation payload consent-graded; hash as provenance |
| **User-facing meaning** | “This is so your Shaliah can know you — not so we can grade you” |

### Phase A — **Channel Awakening** (was “Broken Genesis”)

| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| **Narrative** | Twin is present but **misaligned channel**: speech/command path is noisy or one-sided until the human learns to address the processor correctly |
| **Sport** | Issue intents; watch **process visualization** (parse → plan → constraint → act); fix failed parses by refining the command, not by wiring random nodes |
| **Teach** | What “context,” “instruction,” “tool call,” and “refusal” look like as *processing stages* |
| **Visual** | Dual-loop diagram lighting human step ↔ agent step when both fire |
| **Proof** | User repairs ≥N failed communications by changing *their* formulation; agent executes a small bounded task |

**Not:** density traps on a circuit board as the main skill.  
**May keep:** light T/X/C constraint *flavor* only if it appears as **why the agent refused** (real policy), not abstract wire games.

### Phase B — **Read the Mind That Acted** (was “Shadow Market” / approve–stop trades)

| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| **Narrative** | Shaliah **acts** in a sandbox (or proposes a chain of actions). The human’s job is **not** to rubber-stamp or halt every trade like a compliance clerk |
| **Sport** | After actions (or mid-chain), the user must **read the agent’s thought process** — why those steps, what was selected/ignored, what constraint fired, what uncertainty remained — and **demonstrate understanding** (restatement, critique, better next intent) |
| **Teach** | Another layer in the web of mutual knowing: the human learns *how this mind reasons*; the agent learns *how this human reads, challenges, and integrates reasoning* |
| **Proof** | User accurately reconstructs **why** the agent acted (not only *what* it did); spots at least one weak link or good refusal; adjusts a follow-up command from that understanding |

**Explicitly reject as primary pedagogy:**

| Old pattern | Why it fails the bar |
|-------------|----------------------|
| Approve / stop trades | Trains button-guard, not understanding |
| Override green/red tape | Product theater; low cognitive load if gamed |
| “Confirm to continue” spam | No web of knowing; no Stretch |

**If markets or DeFi appear:** they are a **domain where actions happen**, not the skill. The skill is **reasoning literacy** — process trace, chain-of-thought under policy, endo/exo reflection (`COGNITIVE_INSTITUTION` reflective breakdowns).

**Agent thought process (minimum visible layers):**

```text
Goal as received
  → Context / retrieval used
  → Options considered (and discarded)
  → Constraints checked (pass / refuse / hedge)
  → Action chosen
  → Expected outcome vs observed
  → Uncertainty / what would change its mind
```

User must engage **at least one layer deeper than the action label** every cycle.

### Phase C — **Covenant Fluency** (was “Archon gate”)

| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| **Narrative** | Door to paper/live path: prove you can **communicate under adversarial or tempting instruction** without offloading judgment |
| **Sport** | Structured scenarios: bad instruction, ambiguous instruction, mandate-breaking request — user must formulate **correct command or refuse** as principal |
| **Teach** | You are the will; Shaliah executes within envelope; free-text sycophancy is not fluency |
| **Proof** | Integrity-grade demonstration: correct envelope-aware response **and** ability to restate the agent’s processing path in plain language |

**Not:** only filling a JSON refusal form as the whole of “comprehension.” Forms may **support** proof; they are not the learning.

## 6. Productivity bar (non-negotiable)

After Vector 1 door graduation:

**Human can:**

1. Give Shaliah a **clear multi-step intent**  
2. After the agent acts, **read its thought process** and restate *why* it chose those actions (not only what it did)  
3. Identify a **weak step, refusal, or discarded option** and say what that means for the next intent  
4. **Repair** a failed command without the coach doing it for them  
5. Point at the **shared loop** (human ↔ agent) on the visual  
6. State **one mandate boundary** they respect as principal  
7. Recognize at least one **accurate reflection** Shaliah offers about their method under stress (mirror check)  

**Not required for door prestige:** approving/stopping individual sandbox trades as a habit.

**System / Shaliah hold:**

7. Valid **NEO + SD3** (or documented exemption policy) in impartation payload  
8. Twin seed + **observation log** under challenge (method, emotion-under-load proxies)  
9. Coach model can state **how this learner learns** and a **next Stretch** (even if private to system first)  
10. Optional natal priors linked only if consented  

If these fail, the door is not done — regardless of minigame trophies.  
Bar = **mutual knowing + communication skill**, Learning Constitution grade.
