## Contribution

Philosopher and political economist who, with Engels, wrote *The Communist Manifesto* (1848); who spent decades on the critique of political economy culminating in *Capital* Vol. I (1867); who analyzed commodity fetishism, surplus value, and capital as self-expanding value; and who framed history as class struggle under changing modes of production — a diagnostic engine later used, abused, and argued over by movements and states.

## Key Insight

Social forms wear the mask of nature: commodities appear as relations between things while they are relations between people — and history moves through the conflict of classes under definite conditions of production that no generation chooses freely.

## Event 01 — *Estranged Labor*

**Title:** The Worker Outside the Product

**Passage or Trigger:**
Economic and Philosophic Manuscripts of 1844: analysis of **alienated / estranged labor** — the more the worker produces, the poorer he becomes in his inner life; product and activity confront him as alien powers. (Later *Capital* deepens related themes without abandoning the wound.)

**Why It Qualifies:**
Recognition that industrial work can invert maker and made. The human becomes poorer in being while richer in having (capital’s store). A spiritual-structural diagnosis inside economic language.

**Three-Domain Reading:**

- **Theology:** Idolatry of products; loss of expressive life (echoed in later capital-as-treasure passages).
- **Technology:** Factory division of labor as machine that eats the worker’s form.
- **Cosmology:** Social world inverted — dead labor ruling living.

**Compressed Insight:**
He saw that a man can build a world of objects and lose himself inside it — the less he *is*, the more he *has*.

**Confidence:** 0.93  
**Sources:** 1844 Manuscripts; related formulations in later popular quotation of alienated life vs capital-hoard.

## Event 02 — *Materialist Conception of History*

**Title:** Production Before Cloud-Castle

**Passage or Trigger:**
*The German Ideology* (with Engels) and lifelong method: not ideas descending to invent life, but real individuals producing their means of subsistence — “real life” as basis. Engels later (clarifying misuse): the *ultimately* determining element is production and reproduction of real life — not a cartoon that only economy exists.

**Why It Qualifies:**
Copernican flip for social theory: start from how people get food, tools, and exchange — then law, religion, philosophy. Whether one accepts the full system, the *order of explanation* is the event.

**Three-Domain Reading:**

- **Theology:** Critique of ideology as inverted consciousness; religion as contested superstructure (in Marx’s frame).
- **Technology:** Mode of production as master variable including “entire technique” (Engels).
- **Cosmology:** History as layered process with a material floor.

**Compressed Insight:**
He demanded that thought start from how people stay alive — before it builds heavens out of phrases.

**Confidence:** 0.94  
**Sources:** *German Ideology*; Engels letters/introductions on historical materialism’s scope.

## Event 03 — *The Manifesto’s Opening*

**Title:** History as Class Struggle

**Passage or Trigger:**
*Manifesto of the Communist Party* (1848), opening:

> “The history of all hitherto existing society is the history of class struggles.  
> Freeman and slave, patrician and plebeian, lord and serf, guild-master and journeyman, in a word, oppressor and oppressed, stood in constant opposition to one another, carried on an uninterrupted, now hidden, now open fight, that each time ended, either in a revolutionary reconstitution of society at large, or in the common ruin of the contending classes.”

**Why It Qualifies:**
Totalizing narrative key: social time is conflict time. Ends are dual — reconstitution or mutual ruin. A cosmology of history with a binary engine (later refined, never abandoned as slogan).

**Three-Domain Reading:**

- **Theology:** Secular apocalypse structure — judgment as class combat.
- **Technology:** Political pamphlet as compressed theory-weapon.
- **Cosmology:** Society as permanent civil war under changing masks.

**Compressed Insight:**
He wrote history as a fight that ends in new form or shared wreckage — no neutral peace of classes.

**Confidence:** 0.97  
**Sources:** *Communist Manifesto* (1848).

## Event 04 — *Bourgeoisie as Revolutionary Force*

**Title:** Capital Must Revolutionize Instruments

**Passage or Trigger:**
*Manifesto*: the bourgeoisie cannot exist without constantly revolutionizing the instruments of production — and thereby relations of society. Capitalism as permanent upheaval, not static greed alone.

**Why It Qualifies:**
Double vision: the enemy class is also the most dynamic force modernity has seen. Critique that grants the opponent world-historical power.

**Three-Domain Reading:**

- **Theology:** Pride of a class that melts all solid things (nearby *Manifesto* image).
- **Technology:** Innovation as survival condition of a mode of production.
- **Cosmology:** Modernity as permanent earthquake of means.

**Compressed Insight:**
He admitted the bourgeoisie remakes the world daily — and saw that remaking as both miracle and compulsion.

**Confidence:** 0.95  
**Sources:** *Communist Manifesto*; Stanford Encyclopedia summary citations.

## Event 05 — *The Eighteenth Brumaire*

**Title:** Men Make History Under Given Weights

**Passage or Trigger:**
*The Eighteenth Brumaire of Louis Bonaparte* (1852):

> “Men make their own history, but they do not make it as they please; they do not make it under self-selected circumstances, but under circumstances existing already, given and transmitted from the past.”

**Why It Qualifies:**
Agency without fantasy. Structure without fatalism’s cartoon. One of the tightest formulations of constrained freedom in political prose.

**Three-Domain Reading:**

- **Theology:** Creaturely freedom under inherited condition — not sovereign self-creation.
- **Technology:** Political analysis of coup and class fraction as case study method.
- **Cosmology:** Time as burden of dead generations on the living (the line’s wider context).

**Compressed Insight:**
He refused both the hero who invents the world and the puppet who only suffers it — history is made, but never on a blank page.

**Confidence:** 0.97  
**Sources:** *Eighteenth Brumaire* (1852).

## Event 06 — *The Commodity*

**Title:** Use-Value, Exchange-Value, and the Cell Form

**Passage or Trigger:**
*Capital* Vol. I, Chapter 1: the commodity as elementary form of wealth in capitalist societies — dual character of the labor embodied; use-value vs value. Analysis begins with the everyday object on the shelf.

**Why It Qualifies:**
Method: start from the most ordinary thing and unpack a social cosmos. Microscopy of the commodity becomes telescope on the mode of production.

**Three-Domain Reading:**

- **Theology:** Ordinary bread and coats as sites of hidden social relations.
- **Technology:** Abstract labor as homogenizing measure.
- **Cosmology:** Market world built from billions of dual-natured things.

**Compressed Insight:**
He opened a coat and a yard of linen until a whole society fell out.

**Confidence:** 0.96  
**Sources:** *Capital* I, ch. 1.

## Event 07 — *Commodity Fetishism*

**Title:** Relations Between People as Relations Between Things

**Passage or Trigger:**
*Capital* I, ch. 1 — **fetishism of commodities**: the value-relation between products of labor has “absolutely no connection with their physical properties”; it is a social relation between people that takes “the fantastic form of a relation between things.” Magic and necromancy of the commodity world vanish under other modes of production.

**Why It Qualifies:**
Central gnosis event: ideology is not only lies in speeches — it is **form of appearance** of everyday exchange. The market’s “naturalness” is socially produced enchantment.

**Three-Domain Reading:**

- **Theology:** Fetish as secular idol — human powers projected onto objects that then dominate.
- **Technology:** Price system as interface that hides labor’s social web.
- **Cosmology:** A world where things seem to live and move as subjects.

**Compressed Insight:**
He named the spell by which our own labor returns as a foreign power wearing a price tag.

**Confidence:** 0.97  
**Sources:** *Capital* I, ch. 1 (Marxists.org / standard translations).

---

## Event 08 — *Capital as Self-Expanding Value*

**Title:** Value That Lays Golden Eggs

**Passage or Trigger:**
*Capital* I — capital as value in process: M–C–M′; capital as money and commodities that acquire the “occult quality of being able to add value to itself… brings forth living offspring, or, at the least, lays golden eggs.”

**Why It Qualifies:**
Personification of capital as subject — not metaphor only, but structural behavior of a social form that must grow. The system’s hunger is formalized.

**Three-Domain Reading:**

- **Theology:** Moloch image without leaving economic argument.
- **Technology:** Circuit of capital as abstract machine.
- **Cosmology:** Social world subordinated to expansion imperative.

**Compressed Insight:**
He saw capital as a vampire-formula that must keep laying golden eggs — or cease to be capital.

**Confidence:** 0.95  
**Sources:** *Capital* I, ch. on general formula of capital (ch. 4 in common numbering).

## Event 09 — *Surplus Value*

**Title:** The Secret of the Working Day

**Passage or Trigger:**
*Capital* I: surplus value arising from labor-power’s unique capacity to produce more value than it costs to reproduce; absolute and relative surplus value; struggle over the working day. Exploitation stated as structural, not merely moral insult.

**Why It Qualifies:**
Core of the critique of political economy: profit’s origin relocated into the labor process under wage form. Whether one accepts the labor theory of value, the *problem* he forces — where does growth of capital come from? — remains live.

**Three-Domain Reading:**

- **Theology:** Justice of distribution; theft metaphors vs science of extraction.
- **Technology:** Time-discipline, machinery, cooperation as surplus pumps.
- **Cosmology:** Factory as center of modern social gravity.

**Compressed Insight:**
He timed the working day until unpaid minutes showed themselves as the system’s blood.

**Confidence:** 0.94  
**Sources:** *Capital* I, parts on surplus value and the working day.

## Event 10 — *London Exile and the British Museum*

**Title:** The Refugee’s Factory of Critique

**Passage or Trigger:**
Decades in London after expulsions from continental centers; research in the British Museum Reading Room; chronic poverty, children’s deaths, Engels’s financial support — the material conditions of writing *Capital*.

**Why It Qualifies:**
Thought under precarity. The critique of capital written from the capital of capital, by a stateless intellectual dependent on a factory-owner friend. Irony is structural, not joke.

**Three-Domain Reading:**

- **Theology:** Witness from the margins of empire.
- **Technology:** Archive + long-form book as weapons of the weak-strong.
- **Cosmology:** Global market observed from its Victorian nerve center.

**Compressed Insight:**
He dissected capital from a library seat in its world city — poor, persistent, unfinished.

**Confidence:** 0.92  
**Sources:** Standard documentary biography (London years, Museum research).

## Event 11 — *The Incomplete Capital*

**Title:** Volumes Beyond the Grave

**Passage or Trigger:**
Only *Capital* Vol. I published by Marx (1867); Vols. II–III edited by Engels from manuscripts after Marx’s death (1883). Theories of Surplus Value and other manuscripts further. The system remains a **torso**.

**Why It Qualifies:**
Like Aquinas’s unfinished *Summa*: the map of the whole mode of production exceeds one life. Reception fights over what “Marx really meant” partly because the architecture was never closed by its architect.

**Three-Domain Reading:**

- **Theology:** Human limit on total systems.
- **Technology:** Posthumous editing as co-authorship problem.
- **Cosmology:** Critique of a world-system left mid-construction.

**Compressed Insight:**
He died with capital’s full anatomy still in folders — and the century argued over the unclosed body.

**Confidence:** 0.94  
**Sources:** Publication history of *Capital*; Engels’s editorial role.

## Event 12 — *Afterlife: Theory and States*

**Title:** Diagnosis Weaponized

**Passage or Trigger:**
Post-1883: parties, Internationals, revolutions, and states claim Marx; outcomes range from social democracy to totalizing regimes. The Council records that a **critical diagnostic** of capitalism is not identical with every regime that printed his face — without pretending the texts contain no revolutionary will.

**Why It Qualifies:**
Meta-event: ideas of class and crisis become historical forces with body counts and reforms both. Extraction must hold **text**, **movement**, and **state** as related but not identical layers — same hygiene as Fisher’s eugenics or Tesla’s cult.

**Three-Domain Reading:**

- **Theology:** Idolatry of theory vs prophetic critique of injustice.
- **Technology:** Ideology as operating system for parties and police.
- **Cosmology:** 20th century as field where this thought partially remade the map.

**Compressed Insight:**
He gave the world a lens that cut — and many who held the lens used it as a sword, a shield, or a throne.

**Confidence:** 0.90  
**Sources:** Reception history; distinction of Marx’s writings from later state Marxisms as historical fact.
