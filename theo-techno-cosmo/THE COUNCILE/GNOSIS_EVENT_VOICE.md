# Gnosis Event Voice — Council of Reflection

## Intent (locked)

Events should **look like a life/work listing** but **are not biography about them**.

Essential: extract as if **they are telling it themselves** — close to **in action, thought, expression** — coming **directly from that person** through:

1. **Their written text** (primary; quote and inhabit)  
2. **Known direct information** (letters, documented speech, recorded practice)  
3. Only then: necessary context that does not steal the microphone  

Goal: an **exact reference perspective** for that seat to **speak from** — because the core of the event is **their direct words and acts**, not a third-person essay *about* them.

For each event:

1. **What I did** (act, measurement, composition, decree…)  
2. **What I wrote / said / left** (their words where we have them)  
3. **How I see because of that** — recognition in their voice, from inside the doing  

Not: “Darwin published *Origin* in 1859 and changed biology.”  
Yes: close to *his* register from the notebooks and *Origin* — “I see that…” built from **his** sentences and the act of writing them.

## Primary evidence rule

| Priority | Material |
|----------|----------|
| **1** | Their own published/private text (treatises, poems, notebooks, scores as “text”) |
| **2** | Direct documented speech / letters / contemporary first-hand report of their words |
| **3** | Secure historical act only when needed to situate the recognition |

External sources support **confidence** and fact-check; they must not become the **voice**.

## Stance rules

- **Spine of fact** stays honest (dates, co-discoverers, credit hygiene).  
- **Voice of seeing** is inhabited from **their** lexicon and grammar of thought.  
- Fisher’s “inside” is design of experiment *in his register*; Poimandres from Hermetic text; Bach from ordered praise in time.  
- No mystic cosplay that ignores how that person actually wrote.  
- Cristobal Colon **holds** all seats; he does not rewrite them into his autobiography or speak over their words.
