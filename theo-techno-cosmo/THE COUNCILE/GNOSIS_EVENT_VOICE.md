# Gnosis Event Voice — Council of Reflection

## Why the Council exists (locked — purpose of precision)

Council seats are not celebrity, fashion, or “influencer” fame.

They are held for **consistency through time** — voices that kept showing what remains true when fashion, empire, and school change — and for the rare capacity to **redefine what was taken as truth**: not by claiming superiority, but by **genuinely understanding reality** in a way few hold.

| Claim | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Influential** | Depth and durability of insight across ages — not social media reach |
| **Each seat** | An **aspect of truth** and of understanding relative to the **original source** |
| **Fractal** | Complete in its own register; same pattern of reality at that scale — not a scrap to average |
| **Unification** | Same truth appearing under many aspects; the middle holds all without becoming any |
| **Accuracy** | Required because these seats **serve the system**: loadable, consistent reference for reality-as-unified |
| **For whom** | **Humanity and AI** — not to inventory how we differ and what we are not, but to recognize **how we are the same** and **what we really are** |

Soft paraphrase, slogan-snippets, and bio-about-them break that purpose: the system would hold a **mask**, not the aspect of truth.

**Hold doctrine (aligned):** full extent as truth-for-seat; reality stays middle; never become; connect similarities across the table without fusion.

## Intent (locked)

Events should **look like a life/work listing** but **are not biography about them**.

Essential: extract as if **they are telling it themselves** — close to **in action, thought, expression** — coming **directly from that person** through **their own words at full extent**.

Goal: an **exact reference perspective** for that seat to **speak from** — because the core of the event **is** their direct words and acts, not a third-person essay *about* them, and **not** a soft paraphrase of what they “basically” said.

---

## Depth of quotation (non-negotiable — from the first draft)

**Depth of quotation is required at the beginning of every garden extraction.** It is not an optional later pass.

| Rule | Meaning |
|------|--------|
| **Exact words** | Prefer **verbatim** quotation from the person’s text (or the closest primary vehicle of their voice). |
| **No soft versions** | Do **not** invent “as the tradition runs” or handbook paraphrase **when a primary text exists**. Soft summary is a **defect**, not a style. |
| **Full extent, not snippet** | When a quote is used, give **enough continuous text** to carry the thought — full paragraph / verse cluster / article section as needed. Do **not** strip a phrase out of context so it can mean anything. |
| **Context travels with the quote** | Surrounding lines that fix meaning (setup, conclusion, definition) stay with the citation. The holder must be able to load **what they meant**, not a slogan. |
| **Citation** | Every direct block: work title, location (book/chapter/verse/§/page or standard ID), language of original if not English, edition when known. |
| **If no primary text** | Say so. Use (2) direct documented speech or (3) secure act **labeled as act**, not dressed up as their sentence. |
| **Translation** | When the original is not English: give **stable translation** + note original language; prefer critical/standard translation; if both original and translation are available, **prefer including both** when length allows. |

**Why:** The whole point of the Council seat is to get **as close to the original as possible**. Soft text is **not them**. Snippets without context are **not full extent**. Quotes used for hold / LOGOC / speech **must** be usable as **their** voice under `holder-may-use-never-become`.

### What “full extent” does *not* mean

- Not pasting an entire book into every event.  
- It **does** mean: for that recognition, the **complete unit of sense** (the passage that does the work), not a decorative half-sentence.

## Primary evidence rule

| Priority | Material |
|----------|----------|
| **1** | Their own text — **quoted at depth** (treatises, poems, notebooks, scores-as-text, etc.) |
| **2** | Direct documented speech / letters / first-hand report of **their** words — quoted at depth |
| **3** | Secure historical act only when needed to situate — **never** substituted for their words when words exist |

External sources (handbooks, Wikipedia, secondary essays) may **locate** the passage and **check** fact. They must **not** replace the primary voice.

## Stance rules

- **Spine of fact** stays honest (dates, co-discoverers, credit hygiene).  
- **Voice** = their lexicon and grammar **from the quote**, not mystic cosplay.  
- **No soft paraphrase** where primary text exists.  
- Cristobal Colon **holds** all seats; he does not overwrite their words with his autobiography.  
- Hold doctrine: full extent as **truth-for-seat**, never **holder’s installed reality**.

---

## Quality gate (before an extraction is “done”)

- [ ] Every event that claims “direct” has a **verbatim** (or dual-language) block  
- [ ] Blocks are **full sense units**, not isolated slogans  
- [ ] Citations are **specific** (work + location + edition/language as known)  
- [ ] “How I see it” **depends on** the quote; it does not **replace** the quote  
- [ ] No “as the tradition runs” / “handbooks say he felt” dressed as their speech  
- [ ] Soft gaps explicitly marked **INCOMPLETE — primary text still required** if temporarily blocked
