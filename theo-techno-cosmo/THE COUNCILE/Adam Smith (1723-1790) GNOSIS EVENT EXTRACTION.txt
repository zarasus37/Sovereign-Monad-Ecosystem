## Contribution

Scottish moral philosopher and political economist who wrote *The Theory of Moral Sentiments* (1759) on **sympathy** and the **impartial spectator**, and *An Inquiry into the Nature and Causes of the Wealth of Nations* (1776) on **division of labour**, markets, **absolute advantage**, and the system of **natural liberty**; who treated the wealth of nations as caused by human action, institutions, and exchange rather than divine allotment alone; and whose rare phrase **“invisible hand”** (three uses across the corpus) became — often oversimplified — the emblem of unintended social coordination.

## Key Insight

Moral life and market life are one human nature in two registers: we judge ourselves through an imagined impartial spectator, and we provision society when self-interest is channeled by exchange, specialization, and limited government — so prosperity has causes (labour, capital, institutions), and virtue has a psychology, not only a sermon.

## Preliminary Note

Smith is extracted from **both major books as one system**, resisting the cartoon of pure greed. Credit hygiene: **physiocrats** and earlier trade theorists as precursors; **Hutcheson** as teacher; **Hume** as friend and interlocutor; “Adam Smith problem” (alleged clash of sympathy vs self-interest) treated as later scholarly tension, not as Smith’s self-understanding of contradiction. Invisible hand appears sparingly — not as a universal free-market slogan in every chapter. Primary lines:

## Event 01 — *Kirkcaldy and the Scottish Enlightenment*

**Title:** A Coastal Town, a Missing Father, a Public Mind

**Passage or Trigger:**
Baptised 16 June 1723 (O.S. 5 June), Kirkcaldy; father dies before birth; raised by mother. University of Glasgow under Francis Hutcheson’s influence; Balliol College, Oxford. Forms within the **Scottish Enlightenment** — reason, moral philosophy, and improvement of commercial society as one project.

**Why It Qualifies:**
Seat roots in a culture that yoked ethics to social science. Economics will emerge from a moral philosophy chair, not a counting-house alone.

**Three-Domain Reading:**

- **Theology:** Presbyterian and natural-law weather of Scottish thought.
- **Technology:** University as technology of civic formation.
- **Cosmology:** Improvement of nations as a thinkable human task.

**Compressed Insight:**
He came from a small port and a large classroom — and made the wealth of nations a moral-philosophical problem.

**Confidence:** 0.93  
**Sources:** Wikipedia life; IEP Smith; 3:16 interview life sketch.

## Event 02 — *Glasgow Chair*

**Title:** The Classroom That Wrote Two Books

**Passage or Trigger:**
Professor of moral philosophy at Glasgow (succeeding in Hutcheson’s line): philosophical theology, ethics, jurisprudence. Lectures become the laboratory for *Moral Sentiments* and later political economy. Over a decade of teaching before literary fame.

**Why It Qualifies:**
Method: theory tested in lecture. Political economy is born as a branch of moral philosophy and jurisprudence, not as amoral technique.

**Three-Domain Reading:**

- **Theology:** Natural religion and duty in the curriculum.
- **Technology:** Lecture as iterative publication system.
- **Cosmology:** Society as ordered for study like nature.

**Compressed Insight:**
He taught young men how to be just — and the notes became the architecture of markets and morals.

**Confidence:** 0.94  
**Sources:** IEP on Glasgow chair and TMS composition; standard biographies.

## Event 03 — *The Theory of Moral Sentiments* (1759)

**Title:** Sympathy Before the Market

**Passage or Trigger:**
1759: *The Theory of Moral Sentiments* — how moral judgment arises from **sympathy** (fellow-feeling); how we construct an **impartial spectator** to judge our own conduct; ambition, propriety, virtue, and the social mirror. Six lifetime editions; major revisions (including 1790).

**Why It Qualifies:**
First pillar. Without this book, *Wealth of Nations* is misread as pure selfishness. Smith’s human is other-regarding and self-regarding at once.

**Three-Domain Reading:**

- **Theology:** Conscience as internalized spectator — secular cousin of judgment.
- **Technology:** Moral psychology as social technology of peace.
- **Cosmology:** Human nature as intersubjective, not atom alone.

**Compressed Insight:**
He mapped the inner court where we try our own hearts — long before he mapped the market.

**Confidence:** 0.96  
**Sources:** TMS publication history (IEP); CEI / Daily Economy on TMS primacy; Wikipedia notable works.

## Event 04 — *The Impartial Spectator*

**Title:** The Judge We Invent to Become Ourselves

**Passage or Trigger:**
Core TMS mechanism: we view our conduct as an impartial spectator would; moral education is learning to inhabit that gaze. Praise and blame, pride and shame, operate through imagined otherness.

**Why It Qualifies:**
Compressed insight of Smith’s ethics. Complements later economics: the same species that truck and barter also judges and is judged.

**Three-Domain Reading:**

- **Theology:** Accountable self without requiring constant external priest.
- **Technology:** Internalized norm as low-cost social order.
- **Cosmology:** Personhood as relation.

**Compressed Insight:**
He taught that we become moral by borrowing another’s eyes — and keeping them when alone.

**Confidence:** 0.95  
**Sources:** CEI impartial spectator summary; IEP TMS; standard Smith scholarship.

## Event 05 — *Tutor’s Tour and France*

**Title:** The Grand Tour That Stocked the Second Book

**Passage or Trigger:**
1760s: tutor to the stepson of Charles Townshend; travel in France; contact with Enlightenment figures and **physiocrats**; conversation world that includes (in orbit) Hume, Voltaire, Franklin. Returns to write the great inquiry into wealth.

**Why It Qualifies:**
Comparative political economy begins in movement. Smith sees commercial and agricultural systems abroad before systematizing Britain’s.

**Three-Domain Reading:**

- **Theology:** Cosmopolitan conversation under providence of letters.
- **Technology:** Travel as research instrument.
- **Cosmology:** Nations as comparable experiments in prosperity.

**Compressed Insight:**
He walked Europe as a tutor and came home ready to ask why some nations are rich.

**Confidence:** 0.92  
**Sources:** Investopedia France/tutor period; 3:16 life sketch; Wikipedia career outline.

## Event 06 — *The Wealth of Nations* (1776)

**Title:** An Inquiry, Not a Catechism of Greed

**Passage or Trigger:**
March 1776: *An Inquiry into the Nature and Causes of the Wealth of Nations* — comprehensive system of political economy; causes of wealth (not poverty) as the question; attack on mercantilist “commercial system of Great Britain” (Smith’s own later phrase for the book’s violence against that system). Four lifetime editions with revisions.

**Why It Qualifies:**
Central Economics seat event. Birth of modern economics as systematic science. Published the same year as American independence — twin 1776s of liberty discourse.

**Three-Domain Reading:**

- **Theology:** Wealth explained by natural and human causes, not fate alone.
- **Technology:** Book as operating system for policy argument.
- **Cosmology:** National prosperity as lawful, investigable order.

**Compressed Insight:**
He asked what makes nations rich — and answered with labour, exchange, and institutions, not gold piles alone.

**Confidence:** 0.97  
**Sources:** Wikipedia WN; IEP March 1776 and “violent attack” letter; Daily Economy 250th framing.

## Event 07 — *Division of Labour*

**Title:** The Pin Factory as Cosmology of Skill

**Passage or Trigger:**
Opening of WN: division of labour multiplies productive power — famous pin-factory illustration; specialization limited by the extent of the market. Growth of wealth begins in how work is split and recombined.

**Why It Qualifies:**
Foundational micro-to-macro mechanism. Technology of production is social organization of tasks. Complements later industrial and Darwinian “division” metaphors without collapsing them.

**Three-Domain Reading:**

- **Theology:** Human cooperation as vocation of many gifts.
- **Technology:** Process engineering before the name.
- **Cosmology:** Society as organism of differentiated functions.

**Compressed Insight:**
He watched pins made by many hands and saw the wealth of nations in the split of one task.

**Confidence:** 0.96  
**Sources:** WN Book I tradition; Wikipedia division of labour; Daily Economy classical emphasis on opening sentences.

## Event 08 — *The Invisible Hand* (Used Sparingly)

**Title:** Unintended Benefit — Not Magic in Every Chapter

**Passage or Trigger:**
Phrase “invisible hand” appears **three times** in Smith’s corpus: “History of Astronomy” (Jupiter’s hand); once in TMS; once in WN Book IV, Ch. II — merchant led by self-interest “in many cases” to promote social benefit without intending it. Later ideology inflates the phrase into a universal law Smith did not sloganize on every page.

**Why It Qualifies:**
Credit and hygiene event. Council keeps Smith’s restraint: spontaneous order is real and partial, not a blank check for every market outcome. Complements Hayekian afterlife without retrofitting it entirely into 1776.

**Three-Domain Reading:**

- **Theology:** Providence-language secularized into social mechanism — carefully.
- **Technology:** Price and interest as coordination tech.
- **Cosmology:** Order without single designer of every outcome.

**Compressed Insight:**
He named an invisible hand only rarely — and only as what self-interest sometimes does for the whole.

**Confidence:** 0.94  
**Sources:** Wikipedia three uses; Daily Economy WN IV.ii quote; 3:16 “in many cases” caution; popular myth-correction summaries.

## Event 09 — *Natural Liberty and the Sovereign’s Three Duties*

**Title:** Free Exchange with a Fence of Public Goods

**Passage or Trigger:**
System of **natural liberty**: market largely self-governing under justice — yet the sovereign retains three duties: defense against invasion; administration of justice; and erecting **public works and institutions** individuals will not profitably build alone (WN IV.ix). Not pure anarchism; not mercantilist micromanagement.

**Why It Qualifies:**
Policy core. Prevents both libertarian cartoon and socialist erasure of Smith. Public goods and justice are inside the system.

**Three-Domain Reading:**

- **Theology:** Limited but real office of the magistrate.
- **Technology:** Infrastructure and law as shared platforms.
- **Cosmology:** Ordered liberty as political cosmology of commerce.

**Compressed Insight:**
He freed the market from the mercantile cage — and still charged the state to defend, judge, and build what none alone will build.

**Confidence:** 0.95  
**Sources:** IEP natural liberty and three duties quote (WN IV.ix); standard Smith policy summaries.

## Event 10 — *Absolute Advantage and Trade*

**Title:** Why Nations Should Not Make Everything

**Passage or Trigger:**
Trade theory: nations gain by producing what they produce best and exchanging — **absolute advantage** in Smith’s framing (later Ricardian comparative advantage refines). Mercantilist hoarding of specie and restriction of trade attacked as misunderstanding of wealth.

**Why It Qualifies:**
International political economy hinge. Complements Grotius’s legal seas with economic seas of exchange.

**Three-Domain Reading:**

- **Theology:** Mutual benefit against zero-sum envy of treasure.
- **Technology:** Shipping and markets as wealth engines.
- **Cosmology:** World as trading whole, not closed bullion vaults.

**Compressed Insight:**
He taught that a nation’s wealth is what it can make and trade — not only what gold it locks away.

**Confidence:** 0.93  
**Sources:** Wikipedia absolute advantage; WN trade books; Investopedia overview.

## Event 11 — *Commissioner of Customs*

**Title:** The Theorist Collects the Duty

**Passage or Trigger:**
1778: appointed Commissioner of Customs, Edinburgh; lives with mother; no further major books. Practices the revenue state he had analyzed — enforcement, tariffs, administration after having attacked the commercial system’s worst abuses.

**Why It Qualifies:**
Life irony and integrity test: political economy meets the customs house. Thought does not stay pure theory.

**Three-Domain Reading:**

- **Theology:** Stewardship under imperfect law.
- **Technology:** Bureaucracy as real economy’s skeleton.
- **Cosmology:** Enlightenment man inside the machine of state.

**Compressed Insight:**
He spent his last years taking customs — having written the book that taught nations how wealth actually grows.

**Confidence:** 0.93  
**Sources:** IEP Customs 1778 and late life; 3:16 life sketch; Wikipedia.

## Event 12 — *Death and the Two-Book Afterlife* (1790)

**Title:** Moral Sentiments Still on the Desk

**Passage or Trigger:**
Dies 17 July 1790. Final TMS edition (1790) with major new material — evidence he cared for the moral book to the end. Afterlife: “father of economics/capitalism” fame often eclipses TMS until modern scholarship reunites the system; “Adam Smith problem” names the false split.

**Why It Qualifies:**
Closing gnosis: Smith wanted both books. The Council seats the whole man — spectator and pin factory, sympathy and self-interest.

**Three-Domain Reading:**

- **Theology:** Legacy of a unified human nature under God or nature’s laws.
- **Technology:** Editions as living software updates to a philosophy.
- **Cosmology:** Commercial modernity still arguing in his terms.

**Compressed Insight:**
He died polishing how we judge ourselves — while the world remembered only how we enrich ourselves.

**Confidence:** 0.94  
**Sources:** IEP death 17 July 1790 and TMS final edition; CEI on two-book unity; Wikipedia.
